About Task Tracker

Task Tracker is a simple and intuitive web application designed to help users stay organized and manage their daily tasks efficiently. Whether it’s assignments, personal goals, or reminders, everything can be tracked in one place with ease.

Built with a playful bunny-themed interface, the app aims to make productivity feel less stressful and more engaging. Small interactive elements like animated bushes and a curious bunny bring life to the experience while keeping the functionality straightforward and easy to use.

This project was developed using Flask and focuses on core task management features such as adding, editing, and deleting tasks. The goal is to combine simplicity, usability, and a touch of creativity to make everyday task tracking more enjoyable.
